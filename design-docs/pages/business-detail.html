Technology That Moves Business Forward.

RekanMU develops digital systems, automation, intelligent solutions, IoT, and supporting infrastructure to improve efficiency and connect business processes for clients and across the group.

Technology is one of RekanMU's core capabilities and a foundation for automation and operational efficiency.

Our work spans websites and business applications, enterprise platforms, process automation, AI-enabled systems, connected monitoring, system integration, data and server infrastructure, cybersecurity, IoT, and supporting hardware.

These capabilities support both external clients and RekanMU's broader business portfolio, from building new digital systems to improving, integrating, operating, and supporting existing technology environments.

Capabilities

Enterprise Software & Applications

Development of websites, internal management systems, public-service portals, B2B platforms, mobile applications, and other custom software for business and operational requirements.

Digital Platforms & Process Automation

Development of connected digital workflows for areas such as document processing, administration, field operations, customer support, contracts, HR processes, and other repeatable business activities.

AI & Intelligent Systems

Development of AI-enabled solutions for intelligent workflows, enterprise knowledge, document understanding, task orchestration, information retrieval, and other relevant business and operational use cases.

IoT for Production & Supply Chains

Connected monitoring and IoT capabilities spanning telemetry, early-warning systems, industrial metering, operational monitoring, surveillance analytics, production activities, and supply-chain operations.

Data Infrastructure & Business Process Integration

Supporting infrastructure for APIs, middleware, database integration, server and cloud environments, and connections between business processes and systems.

Hardware & Technology Infrastructure

Supply and integration of supporting technology infrastructure including servers, sensors, monitoring devices, networking equipment, surveillance systems, and control-room or display equipment where required.

RekanMU's digital delivery experience includes websites, applications, database systems, warehouse systems, HR systems, and other business platforms. This foundation now extends into broader work across automation, AI, integration, infrastructure, IoT, cybersecurity, and technology operations.
